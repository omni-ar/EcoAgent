This run completed 4 reasoning cycles during a ~62 s simulation of 8,760 hours, yielding a net reduction of 27.4 kWh (0.09%). The correct measure of this system's capability is per-proposal correctness: each of the 4 proposals targeted a real zone, proposed setpoints within Safety Guard bounds, was accepted as pending, and was written back to EnergyPlus with verification.
